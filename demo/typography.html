02 — type scale

The font-size tokens.

--base-font-size-* are unitless multipliers on the 10px root — 2.4 renders 24px. Derive your headings from them.

--base-font-size-2xl · 2.4Aa Display
--base-font-size-xl · 2.0Aa Title
--base-font-size-lg · 1.8Aa Subtitle
--base-font-size-base · 1.6Aa Body
--base-font-size-sm · 1.4Aa Small
--base-font-size-xs · 1.2Aa Caption
