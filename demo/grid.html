The .pc grid.

A flexbox grid in 5% increments plus common fractions, with RTL-aware gutters and container-query responsive variants. It replaced the legacy Pure .pure-g grid.

04 — responsive

Container queries, not viewport.

Wrap a row in .pc-cq and columns respond to their container's width via .pc-col-md-*. Drag the handle at the bottom-right to resize the container and watch the layout reflow — no media query in sight.
